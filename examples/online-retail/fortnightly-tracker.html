How it works

  1. Ground truth first. Orders are reconciled to the accounting records and the attribution model before any number is shown.
  2. One set of watchers per product. Each product is judged against its own normal swing, not a portfolio average — a small product's quiet fortnight is not an alarm.
  3. Signals escalate. One watcher = Emerging, two = Developing, three = Confirmed. A single fortnight can never move the call on its own.
  4. Slowing is not declining. A moderator separates growth that is easing from a real fall, so the team isn't chasing healthy products.
  5. The fortnight in progress is projected, with a range, from the days so far — a direction, never a verdict.
