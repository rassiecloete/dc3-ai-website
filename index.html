The watchers

Algorithms that know each product

Built for your product lines and back-tested on your own history before they go live. Each one learns what normal looks like for that product, ignores the noise, and speaks up early, on the way down and on the way up.

Slow leaks can cost more than big drops
The pace of your growth matters as much as the growth

Illustrations: two products’ orders, cycle by cycle.

  • ASpike. A sudden jump or drop, flagged straight away.
  • BNoise. A dip inside this product’s normal range. Ignored, so nobody chases it.
  • CSlow leak, first sign. Still inside the normal range, but the trend watchers see the drift. Flagged as emerging.
  • DSlow leak, confirmed. More watchers agree. Confirmed, and translated into what it costs the bottom line.
  • EGrowth picking up. The climb gets steeper than its usual pace. Flagged early, so you can back what is working.
  • FGrowth slowing. Still growing, but less steeply. Read as easing, not decline, and flagged before it turns into a fall.
