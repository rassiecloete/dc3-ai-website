Before we talk

Your brief

Before the call, answer these five questions. A few lines each is plenty.

  1. What is the one problem you would like to solve?
  2. What does it cost you today, or what would solving it be worth?
  3. Which systems hold the data? For example accounts, ad platforms, CRM, shop or helpdesk.
  4. What have you tried so far?
  5. Your company name and website.
